- المقال بينكتب للأغراض دي
	- سرد سلسلة أحداث في قصة
	- اقناع القارئ بفكرة عندك
structure
```c
intro 20%
	hook/clickbait/question
	the bold thesis statement / problem
body 70%
	define/explain the unknown words
	describe the scene / overview / main-characters
	what is the problem? / is it useful/bad?
	introduce some facts/statistics/studies
	quotes
	what/how/why/where/when
	past-present-future
	can we do better/imporove it?/can we solve the problem?
	A vs B
	people's opinion {they say}
	your opinion {I say}
conclution/outro 10%
	the thesis again in diff words
```
