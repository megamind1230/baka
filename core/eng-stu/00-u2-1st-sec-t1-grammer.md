- negataion of {to / for}
	- `not to`
	- for not

# advice نصيحة
## +ve {should} VS -ve {should not}
- you `should` study
- you `ought to` study
- you `had better` study
	- you `had better not` study نفي
- I `advice` you `to` study
	- I `advice` you `not to` study نفي
- `If I were you, I would` study
- It's `advisable` for you `to` study
- It's `important to` study
- It would be `a good idea` to study

# obligation إلزام/إجبار
## +v {must} 
- you `must` stop
- it's `a must` to stop
- it's `nessary` to stop
- it's `a nessity` to stop
- you are `obliged to` stop
## -ve {mustn't}
- you `mustn't` steal
- you `can't` steal
- it's `forbidden/banned/prohibited/illegal` to steal
- it's `not permitted` to steal
- you are `not allowed/permitted` to steal
- you are `banned/prohibited/forbidden` ***from stealing***

## must VS have to
- I `must` study > وازع نفسي
	- Must I study? سؤال بـ هل
- I `have to` study > إجبار خارجي
	- Do I have to study? سؤال بـ هل

# regret ندم
- `should have + pp`
	- should not have + pp نفي

- eg:
	-  I should have studied
		- = It was wrong not to study
	- `He` should not have smoked
		- = It was wrong `of him` to smoke
