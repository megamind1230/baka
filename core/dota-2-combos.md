1. **Enigma + Faceless Void**
   Double lockdown (Black Hole + Chronosphere). Devastating 5v5 control.
2. **Magnus + Phantom Assassin**
   Empower boosts PA’s crit + cleave. Huge burst scaling.
3. **Dark Seer + Vacuum + Wall + Earthshaker**
   Vacuum into Echo Slam = team wipe potential.
4. **Tidehunter + Invoker**
   Ravage into Cataclysm = guaranteed Sun Strike damage.
5. **Disruptor + Faceless Void**
   Chrono into Static Storm. No spells, no BKB saves.
6. **Mars + Phoenix**
   Arena traps enemies inside Supernova.
7. **Sand King + Bloodseeker**
   Rupture forces enemies to stay inside Epicenter.

   Ravage sets up Call Down + Flak Cannon.

   Empower makes Jug farm insanely fast and hit hard.

    Black Hole into Supernova = layered teamfight.
